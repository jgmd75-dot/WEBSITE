The range at a glance

Each category on one page: what every plug-in is, the kind of hardware it is in the spirit of, its main features and what it is best for.

Instruments 15

Synths, organs, pianos, sound modules and string machines. Every sound is synthesised - nothing to load.

G Lab80, G Lead 8, G Bass 1X, G Cosmos, G Glow, G Chip 48, G Voce 90, G Ensemble, G Tonewheel, G Arp, Harbour Keys, G M1K, G Pocket 70, G Rainbow, G Seventy

Drum machines 6

Four rhythm machines with built-in sequencers, from synthesis kits to 12-bit PCM and preset rhythm boxes.

G Pulse, G Deep 16, G PCM 12, G Clockwork, G Anvil, G Byte 6

Amps & drive 12

Amp heads, a practice combo, preamps, drive stages, tape and cassette-recorder channels.

Guitar Head, Bass Head, Desert Ten, Preamp, Preamp Q, Head, Grunt, Model 15, Model 44, PenguinDrive, TS-7, Ten Rack

Dynamics 23

Compressors, limiters, levellers, de-essers, a transient shaper, gates and channel strips - from transparent to coloured.

Compressor, Compressor Q, RND, Smooth, Headsmoother, Quad11, Tandem, Tandem Q, Duplex, Duplex Q, Thrust, Console, Console Q, VB30, De-Ess, Gate, MK3, Strip 102, Field 42, Transient, Twin Comp, Squish, Wingspan

EQ & filters 10

Parametric, console, graphic, program, tilt and tone-control EQs, plus a drawn-envelope filter.

Equaliser, Surgical EQ, TransEQ, Baxendale, Contour, Board EQ, Graphic EQ, Tilt EQ, Skye, Bern EQ

Modulation & space 23

Chorus, flanger, phasers, rotary, panning, ring modulation, stutter, echo, reverb and stereo tools.

Chorus, Flanger, Phaser, Polyvibe, Rotary, Autopan, E-Delay, J-Delay, Space, E-Verb, J-Verb, Haas Doubler, Mid/Side, Echo 34, ADPMod, Chopper, Dynatron, Echomax, Echofish, Ivans Echo, Mod Rack, Oontecho, Squashball

Mastering 6

Finishing tools for the stereo mix: mastering strips, loudness, multiband and upward dynamics.

MasterSuite, Optimiser, Master EQ, Aureus, PM76, Quad22

Summing 7

Summing mixers: route your stems onto one track and sum them through a console, a desk or a tape machine.

M-Sum, Cassette 4, Compact 12, Stage 8, Lustre, Bern 16, Field 42 Mix
